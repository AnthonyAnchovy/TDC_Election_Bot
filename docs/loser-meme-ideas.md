# Loser meme ideas

Hand-made replacements for the loser in the fog, like the Husted cartoon on `OH-SEN_D`. Nothing here is built. Each one replaces the loser's cutout on the card of whoever beats them. Moments were looked up on Nov 3, 2026, and each has a source. A line marked **(unconfirmed)** rests on something I couldn't confirm in that search, so check it before using it.

The figure sits in a box about 125 wide by 245 tall, under fog that washes out detail. It shows at about a third of that size in Discord, so make the prop big and simple.
