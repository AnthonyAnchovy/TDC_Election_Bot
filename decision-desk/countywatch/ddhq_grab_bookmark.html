DDHQ Grab Button

This has to be a bookmark in your regular browser (Brave, Chrome or Edge) on your computer. It won't work inside the Claude app or on a phone.

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+B to show your bookmarks bar.
  2. Drag this button onto the bar. Don't click it here: DDHQ grab
  3. Open DDHQ's House page.
  4. On that page, click the DDHQ grab bookmark. A dark note appears at the top right saying how many calls it saved.
  5. If the browser asks to allow the download, say yes. Save it to your Downloads folder.

Bookmarks like this do nothing on a blank new tab, so always click it from DDHQ's page.
